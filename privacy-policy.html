Privacy Policy

Quarn App
Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how Quarn App handles information when you use the app. The app is currently designed to keep profile details and reading preferences on your device. It does not currently provide user accounts or a developer-operated cloud profile.

Information stored on your device

The app may store the display name you enter, saved Quran ayah bookmarks, reading progress and streak, selected translation language, appearance preference, prayer location choice and coordinates, and Adhan preferences. These items are stored locally using device app storage. They are used to provide app features and are not currently sent to a Quarn App server.

Location

If you choose GPS location, the app requests device location permission and uses your location coordinates to calculate local prayer times and Qibla direction. You can instead select a city manually. Location is not required for reading the Quran. The app stores the selected location coordinates on your device to remember your choice and support prayer alarm scheduling. You can revoke location permission in your device settings.

Notifications and alarms

If you enable Ring Adhan, the app may request notification and exact-alarm permissions (depending on your device) and schedule prayer-time alarms on your device. Ring Adhan is off by default. You can turn it off in Prayer settings or manage permissions in your device settings.

Audio

Adhan audio files are included with the app and played for preview or scheduled alarms. The app does not need microphone access to play these audio files.

Sharing and third parties

The current app version does not include developer-operated accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or a backend that receives the locally stored profile, bookmark, reading-progress, or location information. The app uses the operating system and Flutter packages to provide device features such as location, compass, audio, notifications, and local storage. Those platform services are governed by their respective providers' terms and privacy information. This statement must be reviewed if third-party SDKs, crash reporting, analytics, ads, cloud sync, or other network services are added in a future release.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored app information remains on your device until you clear it using the app's Clear My Local Data control (if available in your installed version), clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling may remove bookmarks, reading progress, profile name, preferences, and saved location. Previously scheduled alarms are cancelled when you use the in-app clear-data control.

Children

Quarn App is a general-purpose Quran and Islamic companion app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated account or server. Parents and guardians should supervise children's device and app use.

Security

Information stored locally is subject to the security of your device and operating system. Keep your device updated and protected. No method of electronic storage is completely secure.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The latest version should be published at the app's public privacy-policy URL, with the revision date updated.
